# E09 — Test understanding and choose the next step

Depends on: E08's tested playable revision; actual participants for completion. Type: product validation. Read [shared instructions](README.md), AGENTS.md and E00's preregistered criteria.

## Outcome

The next development decision is based on observed player reasoning and behavior, rather than the mere existence of policy differences.

## Work

- Prepare `docs/OWNER_MANDATE_PLAYTEST.md`: neutral introduction, consent/recording plan as appropriate, task sequence, observation sheet and short debrief. Use E00's target audience/sample and criteria. Do not coach the desired strategy or explain each consequence before testing comprehension.
- Ask participants to diagnose their club, choose mandates, explain an opportunity cost, play a season, inspect an unexpected result and describe what they would change. Keep manual lineups/tactics outside the task.
- Record confusions, actual choices, whether the player can trace two consequences, and whether they voluntarily choose another approach. Separate verbatim observations from the agent's interpretation. Do not equate stated enthusiasm with demonstrated retention.
- Compare with E08: missing behavioral opportunity, unclear reporting, uninteresting tradeoff and technical failure call for different fixes.
- Append an evidence-backed decision to `docs/OWNER_MANDATE_EXPERIMENT_RESULTS.md`: proceed to deeper people/delegation, revise a named part, stop this approach, or await evidence. Name the smallest follow-up ticket and its reason.

## Boundaries

No invented testers, synthetic interview quotes, fabricated pass rates, or treating an agent's own playthrough as external validation. Do not contact/recruit others without explicit authorization. Preparation can finish before participants are available, but mark the execution/gate incomplete and explain the specific missing input. No gameplay implementation in this ticket.

## Acceptance and handoff

The protocol is independently reviewed before sessions. Completed sessions and the gate decision are separately reviewed against actual notes/results. Report sample size and limitations honestly; no populated observations means no product pass. A positive result authorizes a recommendation for the conditional backlog, not automatic execution of every backlog feature. Deliver the player evidence, open questions and the exact next bounded task to the coordinator.
