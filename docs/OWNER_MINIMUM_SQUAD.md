# Owner minimum-squad recruitment

The role minimums add up to fifteen, while total squad cover requires sixteen. Schema 19 let rivals fill that extra place outside transfer windows, but the owner's director could still offer no recommendation when every role minimum was met. Schema 20 (`owner-minimum-squad-20`) extends emergency depth eligibility to the owner while retaining explicit approval.

## Rule and compatibility

A candidate qualifies for emergency cover if their role is below its minimum, or the squad has fewer than sixteen players and that role has room under the working mix of 2 goalkeepers, 6 defenders, 6 midfielders and 4 forwards. This can recommend someone weaker than the current players; the proposal now says so. Missing roles retain ranking priority. A third goalkeeper does not qualify as emergency total-squad depth when two are already present.

At sixteen or more players, role-shortage and ordinary in-window ability-improvement eligibility remain unchanged. The emergency working mix does not impose a new cap on ordinary owner window upgrades. The shared predicate preserves schema-19 rival behavior.

Approval still creates only a pending mandate. It transfers no cash and adds no player or wage obligation. Next-week resolution rechecks availability, authority, squad capacity, finances and outside-window cover eligibility. A covered squad or unaffordable offer can close without a signing. Refusal and the four-week per-person cooldown remain. An agreement preserves identity and exact quoted terms, with wages beginning the following week. The production game never approves an owner approach automatically.

Migration validates schema 19 before changing only schema/simulation markers. People, cash, history, random states, contracts and pending mandates remain intact. Future recommendations and outside-window resolution use the updated rule. Old proposals become stale; source save bytes are not rewritten. No extra people or rescue funding are introduced in production, and the playable boundary remains three seasons.

## Validation

Validation results and the current package are recorded in [delivery progress](DELIVERY_PROGRESS.md). Eleven owner regressions cover weaker depth inside/outside windows, restoring sixteen, goalkeeper limits, missing-role priority, pure previews, explicit/idempotent approval, pending-save replay, exact wage dates, changed cover/finances, refusal/cooldown and schema-19 migration with pending terms. Rival regressions use the same extracted controlled fixture without changing their policy expectations. The diagnostic CoverShortages test now expects the ordinary outside-window recommendation to be available for a total-only gap.

The optional desktop `--smoke-test --owner-depth-smoke-test` creates an isolated controlled career with fifteen players, a weak affordable candidate and sufficient cash. It exercises the real People recommendation, proposal and final confirmation, captures 100%/150% text scales, verifies no payment or signing at approval, reloads the pending save and checks the actual signed terms after advancing. Fixture funding and forced successful negotiation make this an interaction check, not economy or acceptance-rate evidence.

This unit makes no new long-run population or balance claim. The schema-19 [rival comparison](RIVAL_MINIMUM_SQUAD.md) remains historical evidence: population misses, accumulated cash, longer-career acceptance, clean-machine execution and human playtests remain open.
