## Feedback and disposition

| Reviewer / perspective | Finding | Decision applied in A1 |
|---|---|---|
| `product_review` — player value | Understanding a feature and following a replay prompt do not establish an interesting ownership decision. | Ask players to state their club aim and anticipated sacrifice before outcomes. Record self-initiated inspection/reconsideration separately from prompts. At least three of five must connect their traced tradeoff to their own aim and a concrete reason to retain/reconsider an approach. Clear but irrelevant choices call for revision. |
| `product_review` and `experiment_review` — replay interpretation | A full-season session can exhaust available time; a click or facilitator-led replay can masquerade as voluntary interest. | Predeclare equal practical replay opportunity, neutral opt-out and observable participant-driven action through the first ordinary consequence. Record time constraints, assistance, technical blockage, fatigue and lack of interest separately. Missing opportunity cannot count as refusal or support the zero-replay stop conclusion. |
| `product_review` — economic meaning | A 10% saving can be immaterial in a cash-rich world. | Report absolute savings, duration, signed obligations and cash/reserve headroom, and ask whether retaining the money mattered to the player's goal. Do not create a crisis or claim that a legible saving proves financial resilience. |
| `product_review` — agency | Fixed diagnostic commands could accidentally restrict human choices. | Explicitly limit the four-arm policy freeze and approval rules to E08 diagnostics. Human participants retain ordinary approvals and mandate changes; their sessions are not matched-policy causal estimates. |
| `product_review` — recheck of negative evidence | A blanket missing-recruitment rule could hide deliberate refusal of a feasible owner choice or confuse it with the player's refusal after approval. | Unavailable opportunity means await evidence; an owner declining authorization remains observed behavior in the denominator and can inform revise/stop. An approved approach resolving signed or closed/refused can satisfy outcome tracing; a successful signing is not required. |
| `gameplay_review` and `experiment_review` — development payoff | Extra starts can exceed the existing 25-appearance growth-chance cap, producing no increased development opportunity. | Report per-player capped inputs, the existing 20-appearance threshold, age/ability eligibility and saturation. Keep the six-start threshold, but also require increased development-relevant exposure for an eligible player below the ability ceiling; realized growth remains uncertain. |
| `gameplay_review` — recruitment tradeoff | A lower-rated recruit can still be a bench player; the rating gap does not prove reduced starting strength. | Record both candidates against the current readiness XI and role depth. Describe potential starting strength or cover quality accurately; retain the one-point candidate-quality requirement without promising a changed lineup. |
| `technical_review` — durable financial evidence | Renewals replace player contracts; a later report cannot reliably derive original signing payments from the current player. | Successful outcome evidence retains the original contract and wage-obligation IDs. Existing obligation/arrear ledger references supply paid amounts; failed/legacy actions do not acquire fabricated links. |
| `technical_review` — historical access | Current GameView match/fixture arrays filter to the current season. | Require immutable historical detail by fixture ID or bounded history projection, and downstream validation after save/load, rollover, renewal and departure. Avoid a second ledger or general event framework. |
| `experiment_review` — actual manager sacrifice | The scenario could qualify using only zero-cost swaps, so it would not expose the intended readiness sacrifice. | Require the exact ordered, capped Development selection to include a positive-cost swap at the checkpoint. Keep zero-cost swaps valid in normal gameplay; do not alter rankings to manufacture a scenario. |
| `experiment_review` — schedule confounding | More cup matches can produce more youth starts without a stronger selection preference. | Report fixture counts, starts per fixture, shared-fixture contrasts and extra cup exposure alongside actual swap evidence. Keep total starts as exposure and separate direct from schedule-mediated effects. |
| `experiment_review` — dominance diagnosis | Closing cash omits cash-buffer lows and future signed liabilities. | Include minimum cash and remaining wage commitments in the warning; inspect duration/cover and development/schedule context before recommending revision. The small sample cannot establish global superiority. |

All actionable findings were accepted within E00's boundaries. Overlapping findings were combined rather than creating duplicate measures or controls. The amendment changes evidence requirements and exploratory interpretation; it does not tune candidate generation, selection, ranking, acceptance, salaries, capital plans or cash flows.

## What these reviews do not establish

The most important unresolved question is whether players care enough about these choices to continue playing. Four agent reviews can improve the test; they cannot supply that evidence. A prepared season-two checkpoint also cannot prove that ordinary new-career play reliably exposes the same opportunities.

Sustained return play, wider audience appeal, staff attachment/relationships, autonomous spending, commercial demand and release readiness remain future hypotheses. They are deliberately not added to this task. A positive E09 result supports another bounded experiment, not a claim of retention or a thriving product. An infeasible checkpoint still blocks feature work pending a separately scoped scenario solution.
