# Football calendar and ownership research

Checked 23 September 2026. This is a comparison of the current three-season prototype with English professional football and the closest management games. Football Tycoon uses fictional clubs and rules; the sources below are reference points, not a claim that the game implements English competition regulations.

| Topic | Evidence | Current game | Product decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Season and match calendar | The [Premier League 2025/26 calendar](https://www.premierleague.com/en/about/faq/fixtures) ran from 15 August to 24 May, with 33 weekends and five midweek match rounds. The [EFL 2026/27 schedule](https://www.efl.com/news/2026/may/29/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-2026-27-efl-season/) opens league play on 14–16 August, while a cup starts one week earlier. | A 52-week season runs from late June to late June. The 30 league rounds for each 16-club division run from mid-August to mid-May, with international-break gaps and a festive run. The simulation keeps an absolute career week for obligations. | Implemented: the interface shows dates ("Sat 14 Aug") on top of the continuous internal clock. See [dated calendar](CALENDAR.md). Do not call a calendar week a matchday. |

The current structure should be tested over many seasons before expanding. |
| Transfer windows | The [EFL 2026/27 dates](https://www.efl.com/news/2026/may/29/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-2026-27-efl-season/) put the summer window at 15 June–1 September and the winter window at 1 January–1 February. | The opening forward decision closes in mid-July. Do not present it as the actual EFL rule. The summer search still closes well before the real 1 September deadline. |
| Domestic cup | The [FA Cup round schedule](https://www.thefa.com/news/2025/jun/13/fa-cup-vase-trophy-youth-cup-round-dates-2025-26-season-confirmed-20251306) runs from August qualifying rounds to a May final. ## Immediate follow-through

The interface previously displayed cumulative career weeks as if they were weeks within the current season. It now shows calendar dates for fixtures, contracts, forecasts, saved history and reviews while retaining the absolute week clock for simulation and persistence. Season rollover still occurs after 52 ticks; the first advance of the next season reaches its July date anchor.

The dated calendar is now in place ([CALENDAR.md](CALENDAR.md)). Seasons start in July, the league runs from August to May, the January window has dated deadlines, and cup rounds map to real months. Timestamps stay integer career weeks and dates are derived from them, so no stored value changed meaning. Schema 6 records which season first uses the dated layout; older saves finish their current season on the old layout. Still open: calendar-month owner reviews, 30 June contract expiry, a longer summer window, and non-Saturday fixtures.
