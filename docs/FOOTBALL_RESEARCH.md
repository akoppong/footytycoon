# Football calendar and ownership research

Checked 23 September 2026. This is a comparison of the current three-season prototype with English professional football and the closest management games. Football Tycoon uses fictional clubs and rules; the sources below are reference points, not a claim that the game implements English competition regulations.

## Immediate follow-through

The interface previously displayed cumulative career weeks as if they were weeks within the current season. It now derives current-season week labels while retaining the absolute clock for payments, contracts, saved history and 52-week forecasts. Season rollover remains at career week 52; the new season displays **season start**, followed by **week 1** on the next advance. Forecast figures outside the season are explicitly labeled **career week**.

The larger calendar remains an abstraction. Before claiming real-world calendar authenticity, add actual dates, define the season's start month, map window and cup rounds to those dates, and verify the transition from the final match through the offseason to the next opening fixture. This requires a separate design and save-migration decision because fixture, contract and deadline timestamps are currently integer career weeks.
