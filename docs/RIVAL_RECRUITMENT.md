# Rival replacement recruitment

Schema 14 (`rival-market-14`) adds conservative, autonomous free-agent approaches for rival clubs. The owner's club continues to require explicit approval. No transfer fee, rescue funds or replacement player is invented.

## Decisions and competition

After settling each week's existing payments and resolving pending offers, rival directors review the available pool. A club can have one pending approach and must wait four weeks from its last approval before approaching again. In the opening and January windows, a club below 18 players may replenish a depleted role toward the opening squad mix (2 goalkeepers, 6 defenders, 6 midfielders, 4 forwards), provided the candidate improves on its weakest player in that role. A genuine cover shortage under the existing 2/5/5/3 minimums qualifies inside or outside a window. No approach is planned in the last week, at the 22-player cap or after the career has stopped.

Candidates are ranked by shortage, ability, younger age and person ID. The director selects the first affordable candidate. Wages and annual contract end dates use the same rules as owner free-agent offers. Every offer must fit the 75% annualized wage limit, have no arrears and preserve the reserve target throughout the 52-week downside forecast. Rivals do not use reserve exceptions. One baseline forecast per club review is reused to assess each candidate's finite wage schedule.

An approach is saved without creating a contract or moving cash. It resolves next week after renewed availability, need, squad, wage and reserve checks. Eligible players accept with the same fictional 70% probability used for owner approaches, from an isolated saved stream. All pending owner and rival free-agent offers resolve in ascending club-ID order. This is a disclosed deterministic tie-break, not a simulated auction or a wage-based player preference. A refusal leaves the person available for later clubs; a signing removes them before any later offer can resolve. New offers are planned only after all pending offers have resolved, so they cannot complete in the same week.

Successful agreements preserve person identity, historical departures and academy provenance. New wages start the following week and end at the quoted annual review. There is no receipt for the former club. Failed checks, unavailability and refusals retain their outcome and add no obligation. An unresolved shortage remains sporting risk under the existing manager selection rules; it never blocks advancing time or authorizes an unaffordable deal.

## Records and compatibility

Each rival approach retains the club, named candidate snapshot, approval date, exact terms, resolution date and outcome. People shows the latest eight; all records remain saved. When a recorded former player of the owned club signs elsewhere, the owner receives a dated review. Old snapshots migrate in memory with empty rival history; existing free agents, owner mandates, contracts, cash and random states remain intact. There are no fabricated historical offers.

The conservative policy can leave clubs short or unable to improve. General rival transfer-fee trading, wage auctions, rescue policy, contracted-player retirement and the longer-career population gates remain open. The playable boundary remains three seasons.
